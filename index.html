Reps and sets

Five passes through the sequence per set

  1. 01DeliberateDo each play once, slowly and with deliberate foot placement.
  2. 02Deliberate in rhythmDo each play once more, linking the steps in rhythm.
  3. 03–05In flowDo each play once per pass. Move smoothly from start to finish.
